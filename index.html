About The Project

The Smart Drunk Detection Vehicle Safety System is designed to prevent drunk driving accidents using an MQ-3 alcohol sensor, Artificial Intelligence, and an automatic engine locking mechanism. The driver blows into the sensor before starting the vehicle. The AI analyzes the alcohol level. If the detected alcohol level is above the legal limit, the engine remains locked and the vehicle cannot start. If the alcohol level is within the safe range, the vehicle starts normally. This system improves road safety, reduces accidents, and saves lives.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the alcohol sensor work?

The MQ-3 Alcohol Sensor measures the alcohol concentration in the driver's breath before the vehicle starts.

Can the system stop the engine while driving?

No. The system only prevents the vehicle from starting if alcohol exceeds the safe limit.

Is GPS Tracking available?

Yes. GPS tracking can share the vehicle's live location during emergencies.

Can it be installed in existing vehicles?

Yes. The system can be integrated into most existing vehicles with compatible hardware.
